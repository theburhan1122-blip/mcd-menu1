Types of Coffee McDonald’s Serves

Hot Coffees

The classic Premium Roast is brewed fresh every 30 minutes and honestly beats most diner coffee. It’s dark enough to wake you up but never bitter. My go-to on freezing mornings is the Caramel Latte — sweet, warm, and tastes like someone put love in a cup.

Iced Coffees

The regular Iced Coffee (with vanilla syrup and cream) is dangerously addictive. It’s sweet, but not syrupy-sweet like some places. Pro tip: order it with light ice and you basically get 30% more coffee for the same price.

Specialty Drinks & Frappés

This is where McDonald’s flexes. The Caramel Frappe is straight-up dessert — thick, cold, topped with whipped cream and drizzle. If you’re having a bad day, one of these will fix it. The Iced Caramel Macchiato is my summer obsession; it’s layered so every sip tastes different.
