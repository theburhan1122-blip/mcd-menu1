10 Quick McDonald’s FAQs (2026)

1. Does the $1 $2 $3 Dollar Menu still exist?

→ No, it’s gone. Closest now is the value picks above.

2. Cheapest meal you can get?

→ McChicken + small fries + small drink = ~$7.50.

3. Is breakfast all day?

→ Only select items (McMuffin, hash browns, etc.) — no Hotcakes after 11 AM.

4. Is the McRib back?

→ Seasonal — usually Nov-Dec, $4.99 when it returns.

5. Best value order right now?

→ 40 pc. Nuggets + 2 large fries for $19.69 — unbeatable for groups.

6. Does McDonald’s still have the 2 for $6 deal?

→ No, replaced by app-only mix & match.

7. Are prices higher in cities/airports?

→ Yes, sometimes $1–2 more per item.

8. Can you still get a Big Mac combo under $10?

→ Not really — averages $10.19 now.

9. Best secret menu hack that still works?

→ Poor Man’s Big Mac: McDouble, no ketchup/mustard, add lettuce & Mac sauce.

10. Is the app actually worth it?

→ 100% — free fries every Friday, better deals than in-store.
